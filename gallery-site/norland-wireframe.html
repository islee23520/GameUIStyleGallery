Observed versus proposed

The Windows session showed a main menu, new-game setup, settlement, lord detail, construction, economy, knowledge, statistics, world map, selected cities/villages, pause, settings and save dialog. This wireframe abstracts their layer ownership. No diplomatic order, manual battle or branching-event result was tested. The controls here do not connect to Norland or write a save.

For a field-by-field account of the later army roster, developer battle simulator and tutorial, read the Norland combat preparation report. It explicitly marks the tactical battlefield as not observed; simulator statistics are not a combat result screen.
